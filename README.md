# Evantra De-Buckman Ventures — Corporate Website

This is the live corporate website for Evantra De-Buckman Ventures, a comprehensive international firm based in Accra, Ghana, that delivers complete business solutions across IT consultancy, software development, general trading, and import/export services.

## What the site communicates
- Complete business solutions: **IT Consultancy + Software Development + Trading + Import/Export**
- Core services:
  - **IT Consultancy**: Digital strategy, infrastructure planning, technology assessment
  - **Software Development**: Custom web/mobile applications, API development, full-stack solutions
  - **General Trading**: Market research, trade partnerships, international market access
  - **Import/Export Services**: Customs clearance, freight coordination, supply chain management
- Industry focus:
  - Technology & Software
  - International Trade & Commerce
  - Energy, Oil & Gas
  - FinTech & Banking
  - Government & Public Sector
  - Logistics & E-Commerce
- Brand positioning: Global standards, security, operational agility, and West Africa as a strategic gateway for international business.

## Site structure
- `index.html` — homepage with hero, service highlights, and business overview
- `about.html` — company mission, strategic pillars, and process lifecycle
- `services.html` — comprehensive service detail sections for IT consultancy, software development, general trading, and import/export
- `industries.html` — sector expertise and cross-sector advantage
- `why-us.html` — differentiators, hybrid model, and business-first positioning
- `case-studies.html` — project outcomes, results, and client impact
- `partners.html` — investment readiness, strategic alliances, and partnership benefits
- `team.html` — leadership, capability overview, and execution expertise
- `testimonials.html` — client quotes, social proof, and verified outcomes by sector
- `press.html` — news, announcements, and media engagement resources
- `legal.html` — privacy policy, terms of service, and compliance overview
- `contact.html` — contact form, inquiry flow, and global operations details
- `assets/css/styles.css` — styling, responsive layout, and advanced animations
- `assets/js/site.js` — site configuration, company metadata, and scripts
- `assets/images/` — logo, hero visuals, and branding assets

## Local Development
Serve the project from the root directory:
- VS Code Live Server extension
- Python: `python -m http.server 8080`
- Node: `npx serve .`

Open `http://localhost:8080` in your browser.
