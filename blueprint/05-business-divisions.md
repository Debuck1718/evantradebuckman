# Purpose

This document defines the primary business divisions of Evantra and explains how they work together to fulfill the company's mission.

Business divisions exist to organize Evantra's capabilities—not to divide its purpose.

Every division contributes to the same mission and is expected to collaborate in delivering integrated solutions that improve lives through responsible technology and ethical business practices.

---

# Organizational Structure

Evantra is organized into four primary business divisions:

1. Technology
2. IT Consultancy
3. Import & Export
4. General Trading

Each division has a distinct purpose while supporting the broader objectives of the company.

---

# 1. Technology (Flagship Division)

The Technology Division serves as Evantra's flagship division and strategic engine.

Its responsibility is to research, design, develop, deploy, and operate technology solutions that solve meaningful real-world problems.

Areas of responsibility include:

- Software Engineering
- Artificial Intelligence
- Cybersecurity
- Cloud Computing
- Data & Analytics
- Internet of Things (IoT)
- Automation
- Digital Infrastructure
- Emerging Technologies

Research & Innovation operates within this division, exploring future technologies, validating ideas, and transforming promising concepts into products, platforms, and services.

---

# 2. IT Consultancy

The IT Consultancy Division helps organizations adopt and maximize technology responsibly.

Its services include:

- Technology Strategy
- Digital Transformation
- Solution Architecture
- Cybersecurity Advisory
- AI Adoption
- Systems Integration
- Training and Capacity Building

Insights gained through consulting help inform future Evantra products and services.

---

# 3. Import & Export

The Import & Export Division facilitates responsible international trade and supply chain operations.

Its responsibilities include:

- Strategic Sourcing
- International Procurement
- Cross-Border Trade
- Supply Chain Coordination
- Technology Equipment Distribution

This division strengthens Evantra's ability to deliver complete end-to-end solutions.

---

# 4. General Trading

The General Trading Division distributes products and services that complement Evantra's ecosystem.

Its activities may include:

- Technology Hardware
- Networking Equipment
- Smart Devices
- Business Equipment
- Commercial Products aligned with Evantra's mission

This division supports customers by providing the physical components that enhance Evantra's digital solutions.

---

# Collaboration Between Divisions

Although each division has distinct responsibilities, they are designed to operate as a unified ecosystem.

Knowledge, expertise, and innovation should flow freely across divisions to create integrated solutions for customers.

No division operates in isolation.

Success is measured by the collective value delivered through collaboration.

---

# Strategic Principle

Technology serves as Evantra's innovation engine.

The remaining divisions extend, support, and amplify the impact of that innovation through professional services, commerce, and international trade.
